The brand's section opener: uppercase gold eyebrow, Cormorant serif title, and a gold rule-and-diamond ornament that echoes the wordmark divider.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="Nuestros talleres"
  title="Aprende a formular desde cero"
  subtitle="Cursos presenciales, virtuales e híbridos para cada nivel."
/>
```

Use `align="left"` inside content columns; `light` on cream sections; `ornament={false}` to drop the divider.
